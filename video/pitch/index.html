How it works

Five instructions. One trustless lifecycle.

1
Issue
The SME tokenizes the invoice with the SHA-256 of its tax document.
create_invoice
2
Verify
A protocol verifier attests it. Unverified invoices can't be funded.
verify_invoice
3
Fund
USDC goes to the SME. The investor receives the RWA token.
fund_invoice
4
Repay
At maturity, principal + yield lands in the escrow vault.
repay_invoice
5
Withdraw
The investor pulls the funds. The vault closes.
withdraw_repayment
